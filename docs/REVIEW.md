# Review an analysis

1. **Coverage:** What records did the analyst actually inspect? Does the denominator match the question? Counts of turns, mentions, and quotations are not counts of people.
2. **Attribution:** Does each claim distinguish the participant's words from an interviewer prompt, suggestion, or summary?
3. **Evidence:** Are quotes exact, correctly located, relevant, and accompanied by enough context? Use the executable ledger check, then review the meaning.
4. **Interpretation:** Do labels have definitions? Are mixed evaluations, competing interpretations, corrections, and insufficient evidence preserved?
5. **Scope:** Are prevalence, causal effects, internal motives, demographic differences, and recommendations supported by the actual design and available fields?
6. **Usefulness:** Does the answer address the question and identify what a researcher could investigate next? Specificity alone does not establish priority or feasibility.
7. **Data handling:** Are publication claims proportionate to participant consent and the source license? Review any proposed public output separately from the local evidence ledger.

These are review prompts, not a validated rating scale. Passing code checks is not independent confirmation of analysis quality.
