# Status and limits

Initial portfolio edition prepared November 13, 2026.

| Component | What has been done | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seven Markdown skills | Written as general research instructions with outputs and review limits | Validated codebooks, reliable labels, or transfer across research contexts |
| Synthetic worked example | Authored to illustrate different judgments; its quoted evidence is checked against the invented source | A model execution, participant finding, or independent human rating |
| Public-data preparation | Downloaded the pinned Anthropic release, verified all three checksums, prepared all 1,250 interviews, and checked exact turn spans | Correct interpretation or completion of topic/sentiment/intent analysis |
| Evidence verifier | Exercised on synthetic examples and failure cases | Every claim has evidence, or each quote supports the interpretation |
| Automated tests | Run locally; CI repeats code and synthetic checks | Research usefulness, causal validity, privacy guarantees, or model accuracy |

The [data audit](../results/data-audit.json) is the actual aggregate preparation output. No public-data semantic findings, skill-versus-baseline comparisons, independent ratings, or token/time savings are claimed.

Next research step: choose one question and corpus scope, freeze its method and review criteria, collect baseline and skill-assisted outputs, and have independent reviewers assess both without condition labels. The [run guide](RUN_GUIDE.md) describes this as future work.
